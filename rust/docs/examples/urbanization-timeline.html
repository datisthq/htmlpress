Urbanization: the move to the city

A timeline that doubles as a control. The year slider fills a grid of one hundred people and moves the regional bars beside it; every milestone below has a button that jumps the explorer to its year. The dots are plain HTML elements in a CSS grid, the timeline is an ordered list with a pseudo-element for its spine.

Urban share by region 2025

Tick: the region's 1950 level. Regions move in five-year steps.

  1. 1950

    Fewer than three in ten

    28.8% of the world lives in towns and cities. Europe and Oceania already have urban majorities; in Africa and Asia it is about one person in seven.

    28.8%
  2. 1960

    South America tips urban

    51.1% of South Americans live in cities by 1960, up from 43.8% a decade earlier. By 2025 the share reaches 85.5%, the highest of any region.

    51.1%
  3. 1960s

    Asia stalls

    Asia's urban share barely moves for a decade, from 22.5% in 1960 to 23.6% in 1970, as China sends millions of urban youth to the countryside.

    23.6%
  4. 1975

    Oceania peaks

    Oceania's urban share tops out at 72.0% and drifts down after it, to 67.6% in 2025: the one region where the countryside has outgrown the cities for half a century.

    72.0%
  5. 2007

    The urban majority

    For the first time, more people live in cities than in the countryside: 50.4% of the world, up from 49.9% the year before.

    50.4%
  6. 2017

    Asia crosses half

    Home to more than half the world's people, Asia becomes majority urban, at 50.2% in 2017 after tripling its share since 1950.

    50.2%
  7. 2025

    57.8% and climbing

    Africa, at 46.4%, is the last major region with a rural majority, and its share rose faster than any other region's over the last five years, by 2.1 points.

    57.8%

How it's built

  • The dot matrix is a hundred <i> elements in a ten-column CSS grid; a slider toggles data-urban on the first n of them, and a transition does the animation.
  • The regional bars are one SVG redrawn at its real width; they keep their elements between years, so the bars glide instead of jumping.
  • The timeline is an <ol>: the spine is a gradient ::before on the list, each dot a ::before on an item, and each progress meter a --share custom property.

Source: UN World Urbanization Prospects (2025 revision), as compiled by Our World in Data. Urban areas follow each country's own definition, so a town in one country may count as rural in another.
