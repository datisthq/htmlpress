Examples

A page in htmlpress is a whole HTML document, so it can be anything a browser can show: a dashboard, a printable report, a slide deck, an interactive explorer. Each example below is one self-contained .html file with its own <style> and <script>, written the way a coding agent writes one, around real public data. It sits inside the site's layout, follows its light and dark theme, and needs no build step, framework or chart library.

Every number comes from a public dataset, cited at the foot of its page. Open the source with the Edit on GitHub link: everything a page needs is in that one file.

Infographic poster

Hero numbers, a stacked area by region and a bar timeline of every billion.

Data story

Warming stripes, an annotated temperature record and 800,000 years of CO₂.

Dashboard

KPI tiles with sparklines, a 100% stacked column chart, a donut and a sortable table.

Interactive comparison

A treemap of who emits, and a ranking that flips between total and per-person.

Scientific brief

An abstract, numbered findings and three figures: a trend scatter, a ranking and a range chart.

Fact sheet

A printable one-pager: big numbers, a then-and-now pictogram and waffle charts.

Annual report

A cover, a slope chart, small multiples, footnotes and a print-ready layout.

Slide deck

Five 16:9 slides in a page: swipe, buttons, arrow keys and full screen.

Timeline

Milestones beside a 100-person dot grid driven by a year slider.

Explorer

A log/linear toggle, a year-range slider and headline numbers that recompute.

Linked dashboard

Region filters, a playable year slider, and a bubble chart, trend and table that follow one selection.

What-if calculator

Your grid, distance and consumption in; an electric car's CO₂ against petrol's, on every grid, out.
