Sections and Blog Support

We just shipped two major features: configurable sections and blog support.

Sections

You can now split your site into multiple sections. Each section appears as a tab in the header, and the sidebar shows only pages from the active section. 

sections:
  - title: Docs
    prefix: /
  - title: Blog
    prefix: /blog/
    type: blog

Blog

Blog sections list their posts newest first, and the build adds an index page listing all posts by date, a page per tag and an RSS feed. This blog is one: see Article Sections.
